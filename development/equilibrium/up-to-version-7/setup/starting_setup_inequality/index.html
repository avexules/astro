EQUILIBRIUM v7 - Inequality Model Starting Setup

Players (Circular seating order)

1. PINK (Democratic-Left Economy) - Middle-Class Dominant

  • Dice: L=2, M=5, H=1 = 8 population
  • Neighbors: WHITE (left), ROSE (right)
  • Archetype: Egalitarian democracy with strong middle class, compressed inequality
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward equality conditions, cooperation. Most cards valid early (reflecting stable system), but signature card requires maintaining universal welfare (harder endgame).

2. ROSE (Authoritarian-Center Economy) - Pyramidal Hierarchy

  • Dice: L=5, M=2, H=2 = 9 population
  • Neighbors: PINK (left), ORANGE (right)
  • Archetype: Traditional pyramid - large underclass supporting small elite
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward hierarchy (highest > lowest by margin). Moderate cards valid early, signature requires total dominance (pop AND water supremacy).

3. ORANGE (Authoritarian-Right Economy) - Polarized/Hollowed Middle

  • Dice: L=5, M=1, H=3 = 9 population
  • Neighbors: ROSE (left), BROWN (right)
  • Archetype: Extreme inequality - many poor, vanishing middle, concentrated elite
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward polarization and extremes. Some valid early (high pop, extremes present), signature requires maximum polarization (H=6, L=1).

4. BROWN (Authoritarian-Left Economy) - Egalitarian Scarcity

  • Dice: L=2, M=2, H=2 = 6 population
  • Neighbors: ORANGE (left), BLACK (right)
  • Archetype: Perfect equality but limited resources - controlled low-growth state
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward equality and low totals. MOST valid at start (all dice equal, low pop, all ≤3), but signature requires MAINTAINING equality throughout game (hardest to preserve).

5. BLACK (Democratic-Center Economy) - Balanced Moderation

  • Dice: L=2, M=3, H=1 = 6 population
  • Neighbors: BROWN (left), WHITE (right)
  • Archetype: Moderate inequality, small prosperous society
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward moderate differences and stability. Many valid early, signature requires collective survival (no eliminations - cooperative endgame).

6. WHITE (Democratic-Right Economy) - Elite-Heavy Capitalism

  • Dice: L=2, M=1, H=4 = 7 population
  • Neighbors: BLACK (left), PINK (right)
  • Archetype: Top-heavy distribution - small elite controls disproportionate share
  • Card Strategy: Cards reward diversity and individual success. Fewer valid early (need to achieve dominance), signature rewards water supremacy (competitive endgame).

Total: 45 population

Card Condition Examples by Class Structure

PINK (Strong Middle Class: 2-5-1)

  • ✓ “Any pair differ by ≤1” - YES (M=5, H=1 differ by 4, but L=2, M=5 differ by 3… actually need to reconsider)
  • ✓ “Total population is even” - YES (8)
  • ✓ “Total population ≥7” - YES (8)
  • ✗ “You redistributed water” - NO (game action)
  • ✗ “All players have water≥pop” (SIGNATURE) - NO (endgame cooperative condition)

ROSE (Pyramid: 5-2-2)

  • ✓ “Highest die > lowest die by ≥2” - YES (5-2=3)
  • ✓ “Pop+neighbor≥15” - YES (likely)
  • ✓ “Total population is odd” - YES (9)
  • ✗ “Gave/received≥3 water” - NO (game action)
  • ✗ “Highest pop AND highest water” (SIGNATURE) - NO (endgame dominance)

ORANGE (Polarized: 5-1-3)

  • ✓ “Any two dice equal” - NO (all different)
  • ✓ “Any die shows 1” - YES (M=1)
  • ✓ “Total population≥9” - YES (9)
  • ✓ “Highest total population” - MAYBE (tied)
  • ✗ “Highest die ≥5” - NO (H=3)
  • ✗ “Highest=6 AND lowest=1” (SIGNATURE) - NO (need 6-1 polarization)

BROWN (Perfect Equality: 2-2-2)

  • ✓ “Any two dice equal” - YES (all equal!)
  • ✓ “Total population≤6” - YES (6)
  • ✓ “All dice≤3” - YES (all =2)
  • ✓ “Total population is even” - YES (6)
  • ✓ “All three dice equal AND pop≤6” (SIGNATURE) - YES! But must MAINTAIN through game

BLACK (Moderate: 2-3-1)

  • ✓ “Highest - lowest ≤2” - YES (3-1=2)
  • ✓ “Total population is even” - YES (6)
  • ✓ “Any die shows 1” - YES (H=1)
  • ✓ “Lowest die≤3” - YES (L=1)
  • ✗ “No player eliminated” (SIGNATURE) - NO (endgame condition)

WHITE (Elite-Heavy: 2-1-4)

  • ✓ “All dice different” - YES
  • ✓ “Total population is odd” - YES (7)
  • ✓ “Total population≥7” - YES (7)
  • ✗ “You redistributed water” - NO (game action)
  • ✗ “Most water among all players” (SIGNATURE) - NO (need to achieve dominance)

Gameplay Implications

Class-Based Card Mechanics

  1. Equality Conditions (favor PINK, BROWN, BLACK)

    • “All dice equal” or “All dice differ by ≤1”
    • “Any pair equal”
    • These are EASIER for egalitarian societies to satisfy initially

  2. Hierarchy Conditions (favor ROSE, WHITE)

    • “Highest > lowest by ≥X”
    • “Highest die ≥5” or “Lowest die ≤2”
    • These reward maintaining class stratification

  3. Polarization Conditions (favor ORANGE)

    • “Any die shows 1 AND any die ≥5”
    • “Middle class (M) ≤2”
    • These reward extremes

  4. Population Dynamics

    • Migration now affects CLASS composition, not just total
    • Losing L=1 vs H=1 has different strategic implications
    • Inequality can CHANGE during game through selective migration/death

Strategic Dilemmas

  • PINK: Easy early game (many valid cards), but final card requires OTHERS to cooperate
  • BROWN: Easiest start (signature card already valid!), but MUST PRESERVE equality against all disruptions
  • WHITE: Hard early game (few valid cards), but signature achievable through competition
  • ORANGE: Must INCREASE polarization to achieve signature (H→6, M→lower, L→1)
