Equilibrium v6

Updated 2025-11-25

5th Iteration. For 6 players.

Desiderata Develop interest in sustainable resource distribution and its challenges in a game whose rules can be explained in 3 minutes or understood by reading instructions in 7 minutes!

Equilibrium is about migration of people, the scarcity of resources and its redistribution. Six societies need to cooperate to endure a heap of disastrous events. Meanwhile, every society tries to generate a maximum of individual economic value. But the world community loses all together as soon as one society gets extinct.

Material

  • Population Dice: 18 dice in 6 different colors (minimum population is 1-1-1, maximum is 6-6-6)
  • Resource Token

Start Each player rolls 3 population dice to determine their starting population.

Place 36 resource tokens in the common pool.

Order of player is determined by the total population. Determine an order for the six players (e.g. by rolling dice). Then: Player 1 takes any amount, leaving ≥ 15 in the pool. Player 2 takes any amount, leaving ≥ 12. Player 3 takes any amount, leaving ≥ 9. Player 4 takes any amount, leaving ≥ 6. Player 5 takes any amount, leaving ≥ 3. Player 6 receives the rest of the pool.

Player’s population dice and resource tokens are visible to everybody.

Shuffle the economy stack and distribute 6 cards as secret hand cards for each player. 9 cards remain as a face down draw pile. Any economy card that gets discarded, goes on the bottom of the economy stack.

Shuffle the event stack and discard 12 cards at random. The remaining 12 are placed face down in the middle of the table.

Rounds The following order of actions apply:

  1. Event Forecast Turn over the hourglass. Turn over the topmost card as the event forecast. A secret event card gets reveiled after redistribution and migration. When reveiling the secret event, if it is a crisis, its effect is applied first, then the forecasted event is applied. If the secret event is a normal event, it gets applied after the forecast event. Place both events on the discard pile. If the event card states “as redistribution” or “as migration”, skip the respective action for this round.

    When the hourglass is over before the end of the round, players skip to step “5. Population Check” and play the next round.

  2. Redistribution (single) Players may suggest a redistribution of any amount of resource tokens from a neighbor to them.

    Cost: To redistribute X resource tokens from a neighbor to you, somebody needs to pay that neighbor with an economy card whose full value is at least X. If the condition of that card is met this round, it may be played for its low value instead of being discarded. a played card is put face up in front of the player A single redistribution per round is allowed, including certain event cards conducting redistribution.

  3. Migration (multiple) Each player may send up to 1 migrant to a neighbor by reducing one of their die by 1. Each player is allowed a maximum of one migration per round.

    Payoff: The receiving neighbor can choose to give asylum which allows them to draw one from the economy pile. If there is only one card left on the pile, the receiving neighbor cycles instead (take the last card and discard one from your hand).

    If an event card conducted a specific migration already, free migration is allowed in this round. Dice cannot go below 1 or above 6, so another die needs to be targeted for migration to work with these dice.

  4. Secret Event

  5. Economy Players may play up to one (additional) economy card from their hand, granting the lower value in brackets, if the condition applies in that moment. Played economy cards are kept face up in front of its owner.

  6. Population Check Loss: each player that has less resource tokens than total population turns down one of their dice by 1. Thus, the maximum population loss per round is 1, even if the gap in resources is bigger.   Reproduction: Each player that has more resource tokens than total population turns up one of their dice by 1. Thus, the maximum population gain per round is 1, even if the surplus in resources is bigger. 

End of the game The game is over and all players lose if at the end step of a round “5. Population Check”, one player’s population die would be at 0 (e.g. 1-1-0).

The game ends, and all players win, when the 12th round was played and all players have survived and are in a sustainable state. This means:

  • each player has ≥ 1-1-1 population and
  • each players resources count ≥ population total.

Finally, each player adds up their total economic value from their already played cards with all the remaining cards in their hand that now meet their condition.
