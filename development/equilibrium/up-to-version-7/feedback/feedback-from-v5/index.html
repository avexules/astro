Feedback from v5

Updated 2025-11-29

  • Water should be more of a common pool resource
    • maybe the common pool is where you redistribute to?

  • People maybe CAN get extinct?

    • if in check phase you have <3 water
    • if you drop to 3 population
    • you can get revived?

  • there is 6 different regions and 6 different economies that specify starting water and population and the hand of economy cards

    • Northwestern: Good water and population balance. Migration puts it off.

    • Near East: Low Water, high population.

    • Far East: High population, medium water.

    • Africa: Low Water, low population.

    • Oceania: High water, low population.

    • Americas: High water, medium population.

    • Democratic-Left: balance, dices equal.

    • Democratic-Center: balance, dices pyramidal.

    • Democratic-Right: balance, dices polarised.

    • Authoritarian-Left: inbalance, dices equal.

    • Authoritatian-Center: inbalance, dices pyramidal.

    • Authoritarian-Right: imbalance, dices equal.

  • people that draw in the beginning have more hand cards throughout the game

    • if you would draw an economy card with 6 in hand, you cycle instead of drawing. Your discarded card goes under the stack.

  • maybe redistribution cost should be by any other player but the one suggesting?

    • redistribution should be payed for by playing an economy card, but the card is granted to the person redistributing (or receiving). What about the condition?

  • Emergency Care card is too vague to be resolved

    • Poorest water player gains as much water as their difference in water to population from any of their neighbors (as redistribution).

  • Maybe migration of up to 3 and growth also up to 3

    • migration up to 3 and growth/loss up to 3.

  • Paying redistribution currently needs reaction time, could be different?

    • is fine, I want round timer anyways

  • Migration gets exploited for 1 to 1 trade to draw cards (especially in the beginning, when water is scarce for everybody and people will die anyways in the population check)

    • you will now only migrate when you have pop > water

  • Switching phases needs coordination

    • use a bell to keep track of new phases

  • Some economy conditions are unclear whether total population is meant or whether a single die is meant

    • Check economy cards for “population” and replace with “total”

  • Economy cards only apply to you mostly, maybe they could apply more to neighbors

    • yes

  • Less negotiation on migration

    • Migration should be something that happens automatically in the end of the round when people would die instead

  • Death of people may happen always, even with water balanced

    • not needed since events will do that, and also higher max reproduction (3) makes that there is quickly too much population

  • Or no birth at all (playtesters were using reproduction of population but then realized it is flawed because the more people you have, the harder it gets to be in a balanced/sustainable water state)

    • I want reproduction, else the problem of overpopulation is not implemented and water isn’t scarce anymore

  • After 12 rounds there needs to be a total of X people across the players?

    • I want to reward higher population counts, maybe with economy cards?
  • Economic crisis (reroll variant) is very bad, if it is coming in a late round
    • scrap it
  • Water gets hoarded in a single player, so events will not affect so many people and lose them all water
    • is ok, centralization may be a strategy
  • Sustainable State to win should be changed (also a bit counter intuitive to the narrative, since you need people to die to have enough water for them, doesn’t feel right)
    • ¿no water minimum?
    • everybody except the terrorist can only score, when all survived

    • You can add up economy values
