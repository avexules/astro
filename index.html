Jules Pommier

I am Jules POMMIER, a Berlin-born (1997) game designer specialising in research with games in the social sciences. I bring professional fluency in English as well as in my mother tongues, German and French.

My academic and applied research ambition centres on developing game experiments as controlled environments for measuring human behaviour, sustainability and wellbeing.
